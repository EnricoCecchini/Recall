Hello, my name is John Smith and I am a Business Analyst hired by Marvel Studios.
The Marvel Cinematic Universe, commonly known as the MCU, has earned $34.7 billion worldwide across 6 phases.
This earning, however, has been pretty uneven, so my task was to identify what drives revenue,
analyzing budget, opening weekend, ratings and domestic and worldwide box office to help create an
action plan that can be implemented in the coming phases to increase box office earnings.

The MCU is a hit machine, which has built a complex cinematic universe, interconnecting multiple films,
series and specials across 18 years. Each phase has generated a multi-billion dollar box office, however,
the majority of earnings for the MCU have been concentrated in Phase 3, which generated a 13.5 billion dollars
across 11 films, approximately 39% of the total MCU's box office. Some factors that can be attributed to this
are the large number of films concentrated in this phase, and the high average ratings for the films in this
era. Phase 6, the current phase, is close to matching the ratings, having an average of 88 across only 2 films,
with 2 upcoming heavy hitters with a lot of talk behind them: Avengers Doomsday and Avengers Secret Wars.

Box office openings for Avengers movies have consistently been outliers, with Avengers Endgame and Infinity War
from Phase 3 being some of the highest earners in the MCU's history, as well as having some of the largest box
office openings which have a high correlation to total box office earnings.
Historically speaking, MCU films that have opened above $200M have earned 2.7x higher than
the rest, which brings us to the main point of this project.

The MCU doesn't need more films, it needs more events.

Building up hype for heavy hitters, increasing marketing, building up the audience to these movies as some
grand event has been shown to have a huge impact in box office earnings. Infinitey War and Endgame were the
result of 3 phases of build up, and they became the some of the highest grossing films in cinematic history.

With this in mind, I propose a 2 step plan of action. Concentrate budget and marketing on event films,
and shift release and marketing towards foreign markets. As mentioned, there is a high correlation between
a film's opening weekend and it's worldwide box office, so turning a film's release into a huge event will
increase it's opening earnings, while foreign markets make up 60% of total box office, so a small percentage
increase in the foreign market would reflect as a potential multi-billion dollar earning increase.

Foreign box office for MCU films represents 20.8 of the 34.7 billion total earnings, so even a 5% increase would
equate to about one billion dollars.

The MCU is already a hit machine. With Doomsday and Secret Wars coming, we have the oportunity to make the
MCU feel like an event again.
